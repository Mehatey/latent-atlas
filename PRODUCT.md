# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Portfolio visitors, recruiters, and curious museum audiences encountering Latent Atlas on desktop or mobile. They want to understand the experiment quickly, explore genuine Met works, and see how semantic proximity can continue from a collection map into spatial viewing.

## Product Purpose

Latent Atlas reorganizes The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection through visual and conceptual resemblance. The AR extension lets a visitor inspect a small, verified selection of Met 3D scans at room scale while retaining clear object context and a path back to the atlas.

## Positioning

A museum collection becomes navigable by resemblance and meaning rather than department or chronology.

## Brand Personality

Cultural, precise, spatial. Art and system behavior remain the protagonists; the interface clarifies without resembling a generic AI dashboard.

## Anti-references

No generic holograms, fake scientific interfaces, floating screenshots, decorative AI imagery, cramped museum labels, reversed text, or claims of Met endorsement. Do not imply that editorial simulation is native Apple Quick Look functionality.

## Design Principles

- Show authentic works and system behavior before explanation.
- Preserve legibility and object scale in every spatial composition.
- Use motion to reveal placement, proximity, and navigation.
- Keep attribution and source links attached to the correct work.
- Distinguish the public web interface from Apple-controlled AR presentation honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast, 44-pixel touch targets, visible focus states, meaningful labels, responsive layouts down to 320 pixels, and reduced-motion alternatives.
